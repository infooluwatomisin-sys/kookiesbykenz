Meet the Baker

Homemade treats, made with love.

Kookies by Kenz started with a simple love for baking and sharing it with others. Every cookie, cupcake, muffin and cake pop is baked by hand in small batches, so each order feels fresh, personal and a little bit special.

From birthday cake cookies to banana pudding cupcakes and seasonal pumpkin muffins, there is something sweet for every craving, every celebration and every little moment in between.
